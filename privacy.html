Privacy Policy for Fashionista

Last updated: 2026/05/07

This Privacy Policy describes how Fashionista ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide, protect, personalize, and improve the App.

1.1 Account Information

To enable cloud synchronization and personalized experiences, some functionality may require you to create an account.

  • Sign in with Apple: We receive your name and email address only if Apple provides them, subject to your Apple privacy settings.
  • Email Sign Up: If you sign up via email, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by us in plain text.
  • User identifiers: We use account identifiers to authenticate you, sync your data, manage subscriptions, and apply upload limits.

1.2 User Content (Images & Wardrobe Data)

  • Wardrobe Data: We collect clothing images you upload, along with associated metadata such as category, color, material, details, outfit history, and wear history.
  • AI Processing: Images may be sent to Google Gemini API for AI-based classification, garment extraction, image enhancement, and background processing.
  • Storage: Wardrobe data is stored locally on your device. If you use an account, it may also be synchronized to our secure cloud backend (Supabase) so you can access it across devices.

1.3 Subscription and Purchase Data

  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, in-app purchases, entitlements, purchase history, and subscription status such as Premium or Ultra.
  • We do not process or store your payment card details. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store.
  • We may use purchase and subscription events to understand conversion performance and to measure advertising campaigns.

1.4 Device Identifiers, App Events, and Advertising Measurement

  • We use the TikTok Business SDK to measure app install campaigns and in-app events, such as app launch, onboarding completion, registration, login, paywall views, subscription, and purchase events.
  • The TikTok Business SDK may collect device identifiers such as IDFA (if you grant tracking permission), IDFV, device information, IP address, user agent, app version, and app interaction events.
  • We do not send your email address, phone number, name, username, or Supabase user ID to TikTok.
  • These data may be used for third-party advertising measurement, attribution, analytics, and campaign optimization.

1.5 App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

  • On iOS, we request your permission before accessing the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) or tracking your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising measurement.
  • If you decline tracking permission, TikTok attribution may be limited, and the IDFA will not be available to the App through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.
  • You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings.

1.6 Location Data

  • We request access to your location to provide weather-based outfit recommendations.
  • Purpose: To fetch local weather conditions such as temperature, rain, UV index, humidity, and wind.
  • Processing: Coordinates are sent to WeatherAPI.com. We do not store your location history on our servers.

1.7 Guest Mode Data

  • If you use the App as a guest, your wardrobe data is stored primarily on your device.
  • We may assign an anonymous installation identifier (guestUUID) to manage feature limits such as upload counts.
  • If you delete the App while in Guest Mode, your guest data cannot be recovered.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To organize your wardrobe, create outfits, sync data, authenticate your account, manage subscriptions, and enforce feature limits.
  • Personalization: To suggest outfits based on your wardrobe, weather, preferences, and wear history.
  • AI Analysis: To classify clothing, detect colors and materials, enhance images, and extract garments from photos.
  • Analytics: To understand app usage, subscription conversion, feature performance, errors, and product improvement opportunities.
  • Third-Party Advertising Measurement: To measure the performance of TikTok advertising campaigns and attribute installs, subscriptions, and purchases.

3. App Privacy Summary

Based on the App's current functionality and integrated SDKs, the following categories may be collected:

  • Contact Info: Email address, used for account authentication and app functionality.
  • Identifiers: User ID, guest installation ID, device ID, IDFA (if permitted), and IDFV, used for app functionality, analytics, and advertising measurement.
  • Purchases: Purchase history and subscription status, used for app functionality, analytics, and advertising measurement.
  • User Content: Clothing photos, wardrobe metadata, outfit data, and wear history, used for app functionality and AI processing.
  • Location: Location coordinates for weather recommendations, used for app functionality.
  • Usage Data: Product interactions such as onboarding completion, login, registration, paywall views, subscriptions, and purchases, used for analytics and advertising measurement.
  • Diagnostics: Crash and performance data may be processed by Apple, TikTok, RevenueCat, or other service providers to maintain reliability and improve performance.

Data used for tracking may include device identifiers, purchase events, and product interaction events when used for third-party advertising measurement and attribution.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may process information on our behalf or as independent controllers according to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase (Backend-as-a-Service)
    • Used for authentication, database, file storage, and cloud synchronization.
    • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (Subscriptions)
    • Used to manage in-app purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, and purchase history.
    • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini (Generative AI)
    • Used for image analysis, clothing classification, image enhancement, and garment extraction.
    • Google Privacy Policy
  • WeatherAPI.com
    • Used to fetch weather data for outfit recommendations.
    • WeatherAPI.com Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Business SDK (Advertising Measurement)
    • Used for app event measurement, ad attribution, campaign analytics, and campaign optimization.
    • May collect device identifiers, app events, purchase/subscription events, IP address, user agent, app version, and device information.
    • TikTok Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Local Storage: Some wardrobe data, images, weather cache, guest identifiers, and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Cloud Sync: If you use an account, your wardrobe data and images are stored in Supabase and linked to your account.
  • Retention: We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.
  • Account Deletion: You can request to delete your account directly within the App settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials and associated wardrobe data from our servers, subject to backup, fraud-prevention, legal, or platform retention requirements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your information:

  • Access & Portability: You can view your wardrobe items and related data within the App.
  • Correction: You can edit clothing details at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual items or your entire account via the Profile settings.
  • Permissions: You can manage Location, Camera, Photo Library, Notifications, and Tracking permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Tracking: You can allow or deny tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt or change your choice later in iOS Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

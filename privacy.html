Privacy Policy for Fashionista

Last updated: 2026/05/07

This Privacy Policy describes how Fashionista ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide, secure, and improve the App's functionality.

1.1 Account Information

You may use the App with an account or in guest mode. Some features, including cloud sync and subscriptions, may require an account.

  • Sign in with Apple: We process the Apple authentication token and may receive your name and email address depending on your Apple privacy settings.
  • Email authentication: If you sign up or sign in with email, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by us in plain text.
  • User ID: We use an account identifier to sync your wardrobe, manage account deletion, log upload limits, and link your subscription status.

1.2 Wardrobe Content

  • Photos and images: We collect clothing and outfit photos that you add to the App.
  • Wardrobe metadata: We store information about your garments, such as clothing type, color, material, description, image filename, and outfit combinations.
  • Style data: We store your liked outfits, favorite colors, style preferences, wear history, and related wardrobe analytics to personalize outfit suggestions.
  • AI processing: Clothing images may be sent to Google Gemini to classify garments, extract color and material information, scan full-body outfits, enhance images, and remove backgrounds.
  • Storage: Wardrobe data is stored locally on your device. If you use an account, wardrobe data and images may also be synchronized to our cloud backend (Supabase) so you can restore and access your data across devices.

1.3 Subscription and Purchase Data

  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, entitlements, purchases, and purchase restores.
  • RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID or your account user ID, subscription status, entitlement status, product identifiers, purchase history, renewal status, and related transaction information.
  • We do not directly process or store your payment card information. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store.

1.4 Location and Weather Data

  • With your permission, we request your approximate current location while using the App to provide weather-based outfit recommendations.
  • The App requests city-level accuracy where possible. Coordinates are sent to WeatherAPI.com to retrieve local weather conditions such as temperature, rain, humidity, wind, and UV index.
  • We cache weather forecast data on your device for performance and availability. We do not store a history of your precise location.

1.5 Camera, Photo Library, and Notifications

  • Camera and photo library: We request access so you can take or select garment photos for your wardrobe.
  • Notifications: If you enable them, we schedule local daily outfit reminders. Notification preferences, such as reminder time, may be stored in the App. These notifications are local reminders and are not used for advertising tracking.

1.6 Guest Mode Data

  • If you use the App as a guest, your wardrobe data is stored primarily on your device.
  • We may create a local anonymous identifier (guestUUID) to manage feature limits, such as upload counts.
  • If you delete the App while using guest mode, guest data may not be recoverable.

1.7 Tracking and Advertising

  • The current App version does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • We do not collect IDFA for third-party advertising tracking and do not share your data with third-party advertising networks for cross-app tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • App functionality: To create, organize, edit, and display your digital wardrobe and outfits.
  • Cloud sync and account management: To authenticate you, sync your wardrobe, restore data, and delete your account and associated content when requested.
  • Personalization: To suggest outfits based on your wardrobe, weather, wear history, liked outfits, favorite colors, and style preferences.
  • AI analysis: To classify garments, detect colors and materials, scan outfits, enhance images, and remove backgrounds.
  • Subscriptions: To verify purchases, restore purchases, unlock paid features, and manage subscription status.
  • Usage limits and reliability: To count uploads, enforce plan limits, cache weather data, provide local reminders, and keep the App working reliably.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide specific App functionality. These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase (authentication, database, cloud storage, and account deletion support)
    • Used for account authentication, cloud sync, wardrobe backup, image storage, upload logs, app alerts, password reset, and account deletion workflows.
    • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions and in-app purchases)
    • Used to manage App Store purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, restore purchases, and subscription status.
    • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini (AI image analysis and image processing)
    • Used for clothing classification, color and material extraction, full-body outfit scanning, image enhancement, and background removal.
    • Google Privacy Policy
  • WeatherAPI.com (weather forecasts)
    • Used to retrieve weather forecasts for outfit recommendations based on your current location.
    • WeatherAPI.com Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple, StoreKit, and iOS permissions)
    • Used for app distribution, in-app purchase processing, Sign in with Apple, purchase review prompts, and system permissions such as camera, photos, location, and notifications.
    • Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Local storage: Wardrobe data, images, wear history, preferences, cached weather data, and guest mode data may be stored on your device.
  • Cloud sync: If you use an account, wardrobe data and images may be stored in Supabase and linked to your account ID.
  • Retention: We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.
  • Account deletion: You can request account deletion from within the App. Deleting your account removes your login credentials and associated cloud data, including stored wardrobe files and images, subject to any legal or operational retention requirements.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your information:

  • Access: You can view your wardrobe, outfits, style preferences, and subscription status in the App.
  • Correction: You can edit clothing details, preferences, favorite colors, and outfit-related data in the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual wardrobe items or delete your entire account from the Profile settings.
  • Permissions: You can manage Camera, Photos, Location, and Notifications permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions: You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple App Store account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Privacy Policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
